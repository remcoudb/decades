Frequently asked questions

Why don't I hear any music?

Decades plays songs through Apple Music, so you need an active Apple Music subscription. Without one, the app runs in demo mode: the trivia, ticker and games still work, but no songs play. Also check that Decades has access to Apple Music in Settings ‚Üí Privacy & Security ‚Üí Media & Apple Music.

A song plays the wrong version.

We pick the original version from the Apple Music catalogue, but now and then a live version or remaster slips through. Let us know which song and list, and we'll fix it in an update.

I spotted a fact that's wrong.

Every fact is checked, but mistakes can happen. Email us the song and the fact, ideally with a source, and we'll correct it.

Can I turn off the pop-up facts?

Yes. Open your Profile and switch off Show trivia.

How do I play Guess the Year with friends?

On Apple TV, open a decade, choose Guess the Year and pick 2 to 4 players. Everyone takes turns with the remote.

Which languages are supported?

English and Dutch. The app follows the language of your device.
