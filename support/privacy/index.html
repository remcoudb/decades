DECADESPRIVACY Privacy Policy Last updated: 22 October 2026 Decades: Hits & Trivia (“Decades”, “the app”) is made by DEVELOPER_NAME. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app on iPhone or Apple TV. The short version: we don't collect any personal data. Data we collect None. Decades has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run any servers that receive data from the app. Data stored on your device To make the app work, a few things are saved locally on your device only: • Your listening statistics (number of songs played, listening time, favourite decade) • Your Guess the Year high scores • Where you left off (for “Continue listening”) and your settings • Which Apple Music versions belong to which songs, so lists load faster This information never leaves your device. You can clear your statistics in the app under Profile → Clear statistics, or remove everything by deleting the app. Apple Music Decades uses Apple's MusicKit to search the Apple Music catalogue and play songs, artwork and music video previews. When you allow access, these requests go directly from your device to Apple. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your Apple ID, library or listening history. Children The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
